## 2. The correct raw half-lines, exterior tails and atoms

At a support point y of the original n25 attained law, the predecessor
threshold is **raw** z=H25-y. It is not divided by sqrt(24) for set
selection. The standardized Gaussian argument inside the n24 score is
z/sqrt(24), a different role.

For a positive ray y>=R, z<=H25_upper-R. For a negative ray y<=-R,
z>=H25_lower+R. Both statements concern the same unreflected
positive-closed predecessor CDF. The contact Gaussian expression reflects
H for a negative ray; that does not authorize replacing the predecessor
law or its CDF by its reflection.

The A object has four explicitly requested closed half-lines, one for each
later selected support ray. The B object covers the broader closed union

    z<=H25_upper-3  or  z>=H25_lower+3/2.

Later B positive rays have R>=3 and negative rays R>=3/2, so their required
half-lines are contained in this broad union. The source checks these
radius conditions rather than inferring containment from a successful
label.

For each count/law box, the code encloses raw z by all four quotient
corners of the numerator interval and a positive root interval. It
intersects this interval with [-Hstar,Hstar] before deciding relevance.
Irrelevance requires strict disjointness from **every** requested closed
half-line. An endpoint equal to a cut or exterior/interior seam remains
active. All 25 roots and both closed children of every strict rational
split remain present. Thus atoms at a cut are not discarded.

Outside the window, the source closes both tails explicitly. If X>=-Rm,
put Y=-X<=Rm. For t>0,

    (exp(t*x)-1-t*x)/x^2
       = t^2*integral_0^1 (1-u)*exp(t*u*x) du

is increasing in x on the whole real line, with its continuous value at
zero. Centering and variance one therefore give

    E exp(-t*X) <= M=1+(exp(t*Rm)-1-t*Rm)/Rm^2.

With t=1/3, the closed negative tail is at most
M^24*exp(-t*Hstar). The normalized positive discrepancy is no larger than
(4.9/rho_lower) times this probability, since 4.9>sqrt(24). On the positive
side, F24(z)-Phi(z/sqrt(24))<=1-Phi(z/sqrt(24)); Mills' inequality and
phi(0)<2/5 give the coded upper

    (4.9^2/rho_lower)*(2/(5*Hstar))*exp(-Hstar^2/48).

Both are included in every halfline maximum. The exponential arithmetic
uses the degree-40 positive series and the remaining term-41 geometric
tail with ratio bound x/42. Numerator exponentials use upper endpoints;
denominator exponentials use lower endpoints. The actual argument-domain
and positivity guards are in both check24 implementations:30-37. No
unproved all-threshold n24 constant is being supplied by these tails.
