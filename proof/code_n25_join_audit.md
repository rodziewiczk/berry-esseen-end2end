count targets, their B3/B4 notebook09/10 parent evidence, and collector24.

or an audit of every numerical leaf kernel. Only source, ASTs, JSON, hashes
and small synthetic infrastructure fixtures were used. No archived module
was imported and no real proof leaf, resource cover or numerical integral ran.

in the reviewed current collector chain. The field map matches the selected
original parameter-generation and domination rules, and the reviewed evidence

a bounded source/test conclusion, not fresh arithmetic acceptance or a proof
that every kernel implements every analytic lemma. One unused provenance
constant is inaccurate; its exact correction is recorded below. Runtime and
frozen source bytes were not edited during this audit.

## Checked mathematical directions

The relevant TeX definitions are `rescore:joint-resource`,2591–2630;
the signed quadratic resource form,2680–2690; and the count representation,
support and moment identities,4052–4238. The following code-to-rule checks
were followed against the actual source formulas.

**Fixed index and units.** The parent profile has n=nmax=25 and barrier0.
The original resource export has N25,K0 and the selected literal row index.
Thus the weighted `nD<=N*d_D` interface has n=N here. The mapper copies and
rounds the D cap; it does not divide it by25 or transfer a bound valid only
at another index. The synthetic rescaling regression rejects such a change.

**Nine upper fields.** Mapper lines203–213 match original generator line34:
`D,d0,lambda_max,H_minus,H_plus,radius_minus,radius_plus,outer0,outer1`
map to `D,d0,Lambda,Hminus,Hplus,radius_minus,radius_plus,outer0,outer1`.
Each uses `ceil(10^6*x)/10^6+10^-6`. This enlarges the permitted region.
Positive divisor checks are retained. The original `amplitude` routine uses
a lower enclosure for `ell=sqrt((1-theta)/vmax)` before dividing the acquired
support radii; subtracting the lower p or lower q therefore gives valid
upper conditional outer endpoints.

In particular, enlarging d0 and Lambda is safe in
`(a0+a1)/d0+lambda/Lambda<=1`, because both numerators are nonnegative.
It is not a license to replace the coupled Lambda by an independently
derived smaller theta/mean/allocation cap. The mapper retains both original
intercepts; the count wrapper may intersect its current lambda coordinate
with an independent necessary cap.

**Three lower prices.** Lines214–224 match original generator line35.
Binary/gap/noise prices use `floor(10^6*x)/10^6-10^-6`, with the original
nonnegative binary clamp. Gap/noise prices must stay strictly positive.
Weakening a nonnegative resource charge enlarges the feasible budget. For
the positive-gap ratio, the physically necessary nonnegative numerator and
positive denominator are part of that budget; the direction is not inferred
from arbitrary signed ratios.

**All six planes and both signs.** Lines225–242 reproduce all24 rational
coefficient roundings. Upper A/F coefficients and a lower positive Qbudget
make `A+Fsign*h-Qbudget*h²` larger for h>=0. The final explicit check at
lines277–289 does more than compare coefficient hashes: each of the six
acquired planes must be dominated by a target plane throughout the closed
interval `[0,Hsign]`, for both signs. The difference is
`(At-Aq)+(Ft-Fq)h+(Qq-Qt)h²`. `_polymin` checks both endpoints and a convex
interior minimum when present. This matches original `resource25_core.interface`
at169–183 and the maximum-of-planes form of the TeX resource theorem. It
does not replace a maximum envelope by a minimum of independently selected
resource states. Every field comes from the same selected resource object.

**Endpoints, beta, mean and theta.** Lines243–273 match original generator
lines37–46. Both physical endpoint floors use a downward rounding of the
maximum of the raw and seed lower bounds. Signed beta uses the intersection
of their lower bounds and the **signed** raw upper bound. Mean uses the
intersection of retained upper/lower bounds and a downward square root on
grid10^12 of `(1-theta)*vmin`. The mapper verifies both square-root enclosure
inequalities, ordered nonempty intervals and physical clamps. This direction
follows from `m²=(1-theta)*v`; a theta upper bound gives a mean lower bound.
The additional theta guard is an outward **upper** bound below1.

`theta` is within-sign physical variance, not lambda and not lambda/n. Its
count implication is `lambda=n*v*theta/(1-theta)`. No sign, normalization or
variance-parameter interchange was found in the executed mapping code. One
error-message phrase says “between-sign variance”; that wording is not the
formula's meaning and does not change the arithmetic.

The complete reconstructed row equality rejects extra fields and float/bool
substitutions. The standalone mapper is deliberately **conditional**: it
does not prove a resource object was acquired, validate a parent execution,
or certify that an arbitrary JSON object describes a physical law. Those
are explicit caller requirements and remain false in its returned flags.
