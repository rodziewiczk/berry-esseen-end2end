## 2. Recovering the scalar inequality from the physical defect

Write sigma=sqrt(10), q=1-p, v=pq, s=q V0+p V1 and h squared=v+s. Let a0,a1
be the actual nonnegative inward endpoints; let Y0,Y1 be the actual centered
residual laws with variances V0,V1 and third moments m0,m1. Their outer
bounds are -O0 and O1. Put

    phi = a0+a1-a0^2-a1^2-a0*a1,
    r0  = v*(1-2*v)+3*v*(V0+V1),
    z0  = -q*m0+p*m1,
    rho*h^3 = r0+z0.

These are statements about one physical law, not independent optimizable
moment variables. The physical D half-plane in the actual manuscript,
lines 4274-4280, is

    D*h^3 >= B_sigma(p)+6*v*phi
             +q*V0*(3*p*(sigma+3)-6*a0)
             +p*V1*(3*q*(sigma+1)-6*a1)
             -(sigma+1)*q*m0+(sigma-1)*p*m1,

where B_sigma=v*((sigma-1)+2*p-(2*sigma+6)*v). Substitute the last cubic
terms as (sigma+1)*z0-2*p*m1, then the conserved third-moment identity.
This gives exactly

    (sigma+1)*rho <= D + G0/h^3,
    G0 = b(p)-6*v*phi+6*q*(a0-p)*V0+6*p*a1*V1+2*p*m1,
    b(p)=2*p-6*p^3+4*p^4.

The constant cancellation is

    (sigma+1)*v*(1-2*v)-B_sigma
      = v*(2-2*p+4*v) = 2*p-6*p^3+4*p^4.

The V0 coefficient is -6*p*q and the V1 coefficient is zero before the
inward-endpoint terms are added. Thus sigma cancels before interval
rounding; the scalar numerator is not a new unproved replacement for D.
The manuscript's prior physical D inequality and acquisition of D<=Dcap
remain essential. The earlier
[count engine review](code_count_engine_audit.md) covers the surrounding
sharp-D use; this unit explicitly checks this different cancellation.

For any centered Y1<=O1, positivity of
(O1-Y1)*(Y1+V1/O1)^2 gives

    m1 <= O1*V1 - V1^2/O1.

This requires O1>0 and finite moments, supplied here by the acquired bounded
support. It holds for discrete laws and atoms as well as continuous laws.

## 3. Why the actual inward endpoints may be replaced

For centered Y0 in [-O0,a0] and Y1 in [-a1,O1], expectations of the two
endpoint-product inequalities give V0<=O0*a0 and V1<=O1*a1. Thus
a0*=V0/O0<=a0 and a1*=V1/O1<=a1. These are comparison values, not a new
law, and no separately acquired inward lower floor is asserted at them.

The derivatives of the preceding upper expression are

    G_a0 = 6*q*(V0-p*(1-2*a0-a1)),
    G_a1 = 6*p*(V1-q*(1-a0-2*a1)).

If actual a0+a1<=d and a_i<=U_i, the sufficient whole-path conditions are
V0<=p_lower*(1-d-U0) and V1<=(1-p_upper)*(1-d-U1), with both factors
nonnegative. `pointwise_inward_v2.py:45-49` checks these using variance
upper bounds. The entire coordinatewise path from the actual endpoints
down to a_i* stays in the same region a0+a1<=d, a_i<=U_i. Therefore both
derivatives remain nonpositive, so lowering the endpoints produces an
upper bound for G0. A failed gate returns `OMIT_INWARD_MONOTONICITY` and
does not exclude anything.

Set V0=s*(1-alpha)/q and V1=s*alpha/p. Substituting a_i* and the cubic
upper bound gives exactly the implemented expression

    N = b(p)+s*M+s^2*C,
    M = -6*p*(1-alpha)*(1+1/O0)
        +alpha*(2*O1-6*q/O1),
    C = K0*(1-alpha)^2+K1*alpha^2+K01*alpha*(1-alpha),
    K0=6*(p+O0)/(q*O0^2),
    K1=(6*q+4*O1)/(p*O1^2), K01=6/(O0*O1).

The asymmetric `4*O1` and the positive cross term follow from the negative
V1-squared cubic correction and the inward quadratic phi terms; they are
not symmetric guesses. The implementation's p derivative of C is
6*(1+O0)*(1-alpha)^2/(q^2*O0^2)
minus (6+4*O1)*alpha^2/(p^2*O1^2), as direct differentiation requires.
