## 1. The actual fifth-index implication

The manuscript's `five:lem:qzero`, lines 6194–6213, is conditional on the
**global unrestricted** result \(C_4\le C_E\). On a contrary fifth-index
maximizer it obtains

\[
 q_5=5(R_4(P)-C_5)_+=0.
\]

This follows because the same law satisfies
\(R_4(P)\le C_4\le C_E\le C_5\), including the equality case. It neither asserts \(C_5\le C_4\)
nor substitutes a three-point fourth-index calculation for the global
predecessor. The common fixed-index attainment/contact proofs are at
lines 368–458. Their contamination uses the same marginal and holds the
raw threshold fixed. Compact support follows before fourth or fifth
moments are used. Quantitative support must still be acquired at the
actual index five, using the direct all-index support implication at
lines 492–589; the earlier \(n\ge36\) scale bound is not a license to
substitute its constants at five.

The final theorem `five:thm:composition`, lines 6584–6623, requires three
exclusions: all low-moment resources/count rows, the separate direct
corner, and the supported Fourier complement plus its infinite moment
tail. It remains a certificate-conditional theorem.

## 4. The independent corner is a useful smaller target

The precise domain is

\[
 \rho\in[477/400,6/5],\qquad
 \beta\in[17/50,35139006743001/10^{14}].
\]

It bounds the **positive closed-CDF discrepancy at every real threshold**.
It does not by itself bound the absolute Kolmogorov discrepancy on this
isolated signed-beta rectangle: reflecting the law changes beta, and
the other rows are needed in the global proof.

The analytic argument needs neither C4, contact, extremality, bounded
support nor a physical support gap. With \(p=P(X>0)\), \(q=1-p\),
\(m=EX_+\), \(h=pq/m\), it writes \(Y=p+hX=B+W\), where
\(B=1_{X>0}\) and \(E(W\mid B)=0\). Its conditional variances are
exactly those at v4 lines 6327–6334. Cantelli uses a closed incoming
event and keeps \(T(0,0)=1\). This includes an atom at the count boundary.

The whole-box variance substitutions are upper bounds of nonnegative
actual conditional variances. The Gaussian lower bound has separate
nonnegative and negative numerator branches; in the latter,
\(m\le\sqrt{pq}\) and monotonicity in p justify the direction. The
positive part precedes replacement by \(\sqrt5/(477/400)\). The phase
partition covers \([0,5]\); the separately derived bounds cover
\(z\le0\) and \(z\ge5\). The map \(H\mapsto5p+hH\) is onto since
\(h>0\). These points justify the conditional whole-line implication
before any leaf values are computed.

There is a real implementation, rather than only a proposed tree rule.
The archived kernel uses twelve closed beta slabs and five compact
phase roots per slab. The fixed forest has 60 roots, 1,994 nodes and
1,027 leaves. The independent reader reconstructs all root domains,
evaluates a separate rational Gaussian enclosure on every leaf, and
reconstructs both exterior comparisons. This review read its formulas
and checked their bound directions against v4, but did not execute them.
