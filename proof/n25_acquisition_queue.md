## Unresolved selectors, payloads and execution contracts

1. The exact full lowerB3/lowerB4 regional records and two companion selection
   files are absent under their declared names in the two principal archives.
   [The B3/B4 catalog](n25_retained_b34_catalog.md) identifies the four hard
   links and the provisional B3p34 selection. A final dependency map alone is
   not a replacement for those regional outcome/coverage selectors.
2. B3p30 selects the supplemental S1 resource route. B3p31/p32 use the V14
   same-witness local-rho feedback hierarchy, including three rounds and
   denominator10^9 rounding. Ordinary whole B3 global resources do not
   discharge either distinction. The current seven reconstruction jobs remain
   needed on their scoped routes; this audit neither replaces nor restarts
   them.
3. The selected lowerB5 ordinary V14 parents p26–32 and four p33 V12 quarters
   are missing under their exact source names. The p24/p25 mixed candidates
   and full acceptor sources are supplied in base13, but their complete
   acquisition/theorem/scalar registries and absent original V14 parent
   bindings still require explicit replay/interface specifications.
4. The upper catalog's ten transitive count references are not all established
   as a minimal final selector. The middle-B3 composer explicitly identifies
   its three-count collection; the remaining upper selector/closure work must
   not be replaced by running every inherited source-graph forest.
5. Several noncount actions call `resource_exact/resource_worker.py` through
   subprocesses; cache wrappers may launch another interpreter as well. The
   current single-process native guard is unsuitable for those unchanged
   process trees. This is an execution-contract issue, not evidence of a
   mathematical failure or an uncomputable inequality. Unchanged full
   arithmetic can run under an explicitly described BASIC tree supervisor,
   with exact source closure, complete child/capture census, full controls and
   honest `native_arithmetic_accepted=false`. A stronger audited process-tree
   guard or an explicit equivalent single-process port is a separate route.
6. Archived analytic reviews, support/cache identities, universal predecessor
   arguments, same-law resource implications and closed seam coverage remain
   mathematical premises to review. A source hash or a supervisor guard tag
   cannot establish them.

## Compact packaging recommendation

Keep the already published retained count batches separate from parent
acquisition. A useful next packaging sequence is: one small B0–B2 parent batch
with three distinct parent chains and two local continuations; a retained
B3/B4 count batch alongside explicit S1/V14 interface work; one middle B4/B5
batch with canonical parents followed by separately sealed selected locals;
and one upper acquisition batch partitioned by the literal domain/source
families above. Mixed B5 and missing-payload replacements need their own
explicit designs before inclusion. Batch grouping is an operational choice,
not a total count of required notebooks or proof obligations.

Resume only a stage whose complete result has been revalidated against its
full source/contract/domain/parent identity, actual captures and execution
record. Reuse validated parents by those identities, not by matching file
names. This avoids repeating legitimate shared work without treating an old
conditional receipt or a nearby source capsule as an acquired theorem.

Fresh arithmetic in this audit, complete acquisition-closure validation,
complete regional selection, full n25 acceptance and all-index conclusion are
all **false**. Notebook16's supplied completion log is separately observed
progress; this document makes no claim to have validated its result ZIP.
