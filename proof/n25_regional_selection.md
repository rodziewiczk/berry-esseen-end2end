# Selected regional inputs of the historical n25 union

The companion [JSON inventory](n25_regional_selection.json) records the exact
regional input selection of the archived final n25 composer. It was built by
reading the original selection/dependency records and hashing selected ZIP
member bytes. No mathematical module was imported or executed.

The selection has13 inputs: the auxiliary24 predecessor, lower branches B0–B4,
the final selected B5 union, the retained upper/high/middle union and its review,
two middle-beta branches, the signed-beta ceiling and the scalar-scope record.
The historical dependency inventory declares60 nodes; that broader provenance
inventory is not a minimal list of native computations.

Eleven of the13 exact selected members were found under their declared paths
in the two principal source archives and matched their historical SHA-256
expectations. The declared `lowerB3` and `lowerB4` member paths were not located
there. This is a bounded literal-path finding. It neither rules out equivalent
data elsewhere nor requires recreating old report bytes: fresh compositions
must instead be rebuilt from their actual component results and justified
mathematical implications.

Each located input records its member size, actual digest, restricted historical
domain/accounting fields and, where present, its outcome-method census.
Historical statuses and counts are provenance. In particular, source graph
reference counts contain inherited and overlapping objects; they must not be
added or treated as a fresh leaf-replay census.

All published paths are archive-relative, with personal source prefixes removed.
No private account log or local absolute path is needed to identify these source
members. The companion JSON keeps `all_leaf_jobs_enumerated=false`,
`fresh_mathematical_execution=false`, `full_n25_verified=false` and
`all_index_conclusion_verified=false`.

This is a precise regional selection, not a finished executable catalog for all
remaining n25 native jobs. The next catalog must descend through these selected
regional outcomes to their exact support/Fourier/INITIAL/resource/count tasks,
preserve every replacement and closed seam, and separate existing candidates
from the seven missing forests. The dedicated notebook24 addresses the latter
seven interfaces; see [its scope](n25_collect_execution.md) and
[the remaining n25 obligations](n25_completion_scope.md).
