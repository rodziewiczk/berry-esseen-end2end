(-A,B). Put `c=sqrt(sigma+1)`, `e=sqrt(sigma-1)`, `C0=c+e` and

`w^2<=3.7d`. Also AB<=1. Direct solution gives

```

```

Consequently `initial_cell:107–109` computes an upper h_* for A+B and

`|J1|<=alpha*(rho+1)+(3-alpha)*d/2`,

`J<=rho+1` and `J_outer<=T<=d/2`. The sign of3-alpha is checked before
