## 6. The two additional elementary6--9 rules

The sign-mass exclusion at6 is valid for arbitrary laws, independently of
the Berry--Esseen reduction. Set a=E X_+^2,1-a=E X_-^2 and m=E X_+>0.

`rho>=[a^2+(1-a)^2]/m`, hence

`p*rho^2 >= [a^2+(1-a)^2]^2/a`.

On0<a<1, differentiation has the sign of `6a^2-2a-1`, since its remaining
factor is positive. The unique positive stationary point is
`a=(1+sqrt(7))/6`, and the endpoint behavior leaves it as the global minimum
`(8/27)*(7*sqrt(7)-17)>11261/25000`. The same proof applies to the other
sign probability and permits an atom at zero. Multiplying a whole p panel's

left side. A **strict** upper below11261/25000 excludes the whole closed
panel. The source restricts this rule to the declared6 panels:35/36 in B0,
32/33 in BN and30/31 in B2. It is not borrowed at7--9 without acquisition.

`lambda=n*(v^2/m^2-v)`, v=p(1-p). Replacing m by its positive lower bound
is outward; this expression is convex in v, so its maximum over a closed

charge. The transformed threshold z=np+(v/m)*H must still be acquired
