# Fresh parent/count evidence and seven n25 interfaces

The queued notebook24 is an evidence and exact rational contract join. It reads
the completed results from notebooks01–07 and09/10 on the same Drive account.
It does not rerun their searches, numerical leaves, INITIAL covers or resource
calculations. Previous execution-code commits and exported bytes are retained.

## Required inputs

The selected jobs are `n25-parents-b3`, `n25-parents-b4`, `b3-p33`, `b4-q0`
through `b4-q3`, and the two explicitly new conditional portable V14 targets
`b3-p31-v14-new` and `b3-p32-v14-new`.

Each imported ZIP must have its actual SHA-256 sidecar. The collector reuses the
bounded extraction protocol in `aux24_join.py`: one original root, ordinary
files, finite expansion, no unsafe paths or links, and unchanged archive bytes
before and after extraction. It then calls the dedicated count/parent evidence
validators. A saved filename or PASS label alone is insufficient.

The count validator requires the source-bound native acceptance, actual native
execution/guard/captures, all26 original roots, the complete leaf census and all24
corruption controls. The parent validator requires all four original stages,
full INITIAL/resource domains, complete controls and their actual fresh input
edges. These checks map preserved original VM paths to restored artifacts while
leaving argv, native receipts, captures and proof objects unchanged.

The returned report, its hash, validator source identity and source ZIP hash are
recorded for every import. The collector additionally binds the actual count
acceptance report bytes and the exact contract/resource proof bytes passed to
the semantic mapper.

## Simultaneous resource-to-count implications

For each count target the collector selects its freshly validated whole B3 or B4
resource proof. `n25_contract_interfaces.validate` checks the exact simultaneous
mapping from the selected surviving resource row to the frozen count contract.
This includes the original outward rounding, support and threshold bounds,
affine prices, signed quadratic inequalities and acquired moment information.
The derivation and field coverage are documented in the mapper's scoped review.

The four B4 targets are closed probability quarters with endpoints
`29/100,117/400,59/200,119/400,3/10`. Their union is the complete original p29
interval. The B3 target endpoints are `31/100,8/25,33/100,17/50`; shared seams
belong to both adjacent closed intervals. The collector checks these exact
selected covers rather than treating the four quarters as duplicate whole-panel
jobs or omitting seam endpoints.

The two portable V14 contracts also require their byte-bound original V12
reference and the precisely specified two additional contact fields. The mapper
does not turn that attained-contact assertion into a theorem by checking its
dictionary. Its same-witness analytic implication and the acquired global
auxiliary24 statement remain explicit premises.

Historical resource-acceptance hashes in a frozen count contract are provenance
inputs. Fresh resource receipts can have different elapsed-time bytes. The new
join validates actual fresh parent evidence and the numeric implication to the
unchanged count contract; it neither rewrites old receipts nor inserts an old
accepted receipt to satisfy a historical identity.

## Results and use

Upload notebook24 to Colab, select a CPU runtime and choose Run all. No parameters
need editing. It finds completed bundles under their original task directories
in `MyDrive/Berry-Esseen-end2end/`. Keep one instance of this collector per Drive.

Compatible complete exports are considered newest first; incomplete or invalid
exports are recorded and skipped. Available individual interfaces can be checked
while other jobs remain missing. The final state is `WAITING_FOR_N25_EVIDENCE`
until both parents and all seven count exports validate. Repeating this evidence
join after another job finishes does not repeat the expensive mathematics.

`CONDITIONAL_SEVEN_N25_COUNT_INTERFACES_VERIFIED` means all seven selected
interfaces and their closed seams passed. It does **not** complete n25. The
other n25 probability panels, B0/B1/B2/B5 branches, upper/middle/high domains,
external inputs and all-law composition remain separate; see
[the remaining n25 scope](n25_completion_scope.md).

The report keeps `stronger24_acquisition_verified`,
`analytic_attained_contact_premise_acquired`, `full_n25_verified`,
`all_index_conclusion_verified` and whole-collector `native_arithmetic_accepted`
false. Individual imported native acceptances retain their actual scoped status.
Exact contract comparisons occur in the Colab collector, outside a new trusted
native-leaf run; execution contracts and mathematical implications are recorded
separately.

Progress identifies imports, rejected evidence and verified interfaces. Live
copies run every60 seconds. A complete, waiting or handled-error result saves a
final ZIP/SHA sidecar. Local tests use synthetic transport and mocked arithmetic;
they do not establish a fresh proof calculation.
