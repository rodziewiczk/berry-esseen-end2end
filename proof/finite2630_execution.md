## Resource and evidence contract

Each of the79 full acceptors runs under the existing BASIC Linux process-group
supervisor with CPU43200seconds, wall54000seconds,2GiB per-process address-space
limit and sampled live-group RSS limit,512MiB sampled file budget,16MiB captured
log budget, and20second heartbeat. CPU enforcement is per-process plus sampled
live-group CPU, not cumulative descendant CPU accounting. This is not a Windows
Job or cgroup equivalence claim and is never promoted to native proof acceptance.
The imported64 pair retains its own original7200CPU/7260wall/2GiB policy.

Completion requires exact integer return code0, the actual stopped supervisor
result and launch command/cwd/output/limits, unchanged sealed source bytes, actual
stdout/stderr SHA values, empty successful stderr, terminal output equal to the
whole fresh native receipt, the stopped whole-output copy, complete native
statistics/control labels, and the exact source-selected forest census. The
observed report is explicitly false before validation; the completed ledger is
written only after the full validator succeeds and is then independently read
back. Completed-stage ZIPs have safe bounded inventories and actual SHA sidecars.

The cache namespace `finite2630-counts/completed/<selection-prefix>` is new.
Only fully revalidated completed stages are reused. Interrupted native stages
run again in a fresh private capsule; partial numerical work is not resumed.
Existing completed stages and all prior ZIPs are retained. Progress is streamed
and logged every20seconds; advisory Drive logs are backed up every60seconds.
Each completed stage is exported separately. A final ZIP is exported on failure
as well as success, with bounded original stderr diagnostics where available.

After all79 jobs, `validate_batch` independently revalidates every completed
stage and the imported64 pair, requires the exact ordered80-forest selection
and full census, and only then writes `BATCH_VALIDATED.json`. A failed final join
revokes proposed completion. `SESSION_RESULT.json` remains byte-stable after its
successful validation so its recorded SHA can be checked after export.

`validate_completed(task, original_task_root=None)` and `validate_batch(root)`
perform pure source/process/evidence readback. They do not import or rerun an
original mathematical kernel. Sources and actual recorded paths remain bound
to their original contexts even after restoring the bundle elsewhere.

## Limits and remaining proof obligations

This protocol can establish only full selected conditional COUNT arithmetic and
the exact recovered candidate identity edge. INITIAL/resource/support/Fourier,
same-law witness acquisition, predecessor bounds, closed regional composition,
and the complete theorem remain separate obligations. In every stage and final
result, `native_arithmetic_accepted`, `parent_acquisitions_verified`,
`fresh_parent_fact_joins_verified`, `full_finite1039_verified`, and
`all_index_conclusion_verified` remain false. No historical PASS receipt is used
as a freshly acquired mathematical premise.

Local verification consists of source/hash/JSON preparation and synthetic
infrastructure fixtures with trap numerical files. `proof/finite2630_source_preparation.json`
records the actual79 complete source preparations and structural candidate
censuses. This is not an arithmetic replay or external independent verification.
